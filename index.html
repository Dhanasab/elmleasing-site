Questions

Straight answers.

Does it cost anything for case managers or tenants?

No. Case managers, navigators and their clients never pay us anything. Landlords pay a placement fee only after a tenant moves in.

Why don't you list units on the site?

Voucher units rent fast, so a public list is out of date by the time you read it. Tell us what your client needs and we'll send you what's actually open right now, matched to the voucher.

Which voucher programs do you accept?

All of them: Section 8 / HCV through HACLA, LACDA and city housing authorities, HUD-VASH, EHV, TLS, Brilliant Corners, HOM, DMH and others. Not sure? Ask us.

What does "one-stop shop" mean?

You tell us once what your client needs: unit type, ADA accessible or not, location, budget and move-in date. We find the units, book the tours, submit the application and RFTA, schedule the inspection and get the lease signed. You don't have to call around to landlords.

How fast can a client move in?

We send matching units within 24 hours of your request, and tours usually happen within days. After that, move-in timing depends mostly on the housing authority's RFTA and inspection schedule, which we push as hard as we can.

Can you find ADA accessible units?

Yes. Choose the accessibility option your client needs on the form, such as a wheelchair accessible unit or ground floor, and we'll only send units that fit.

I'm a landlord. Is this exclusive?

No. Keep advertising your unit however you like. If you rent it to someone we didn't bring, you owe us nothing.

What do landlords pay, and when?

One placement fee, due after the tenant moves in. Nothing upfront and no monthly fees. We'll confirm the exact terms in a short one-page agreement before we start.

Where do you work?

All of Southern California: Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, Ventura and San Diego counties.
